Forging a Sustainable Future Since 2008

SOS Packaging was founded with a clear mission: to disrupt the traditional reliance on wood in industrial packaging. We recognized early on that durability shouldn't come at the cost of our forests.

By engineering high-quality, reusable steel drums and spools, we provide our B2B partners with packaging solutions that are stronger, safer, and inherently sustainable. Our "Save Wood, Go Green" philosophy drives every aspect of our manufacturing process.
